AgentEasy Privacy Policy

Effective August 14, 2026

AgentEasy is built by Brooklyn Group for real estate agents to manage their leads, showings, units, and client contacts in one place. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and what choices you have. If anything here is unclear, email us at support@hazbot.app.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an AgentEasy account we collect your name, email address, and password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your plaintext password). If you provide it, we also store your brokerage name.

Your connected Gmail account (optional). If you choose to connect your Gmail inbox, AgentEasy requests permission to read your inbox and send email as you. We use this to detect new lead inquiries from listing sites (like StreetEasy or Zillow), to pick up your clients' replies, to send auto-replies and follow-ups you've configured, and to send replies you write yourself from within the app using your own Gmail signature. Google's permission grant covers your inbox broadly, but AgentEasy's own logic only processes messages from known listing-site senders and the reply threads that follow from them — we don't read or store the rest of your inbox. You can disconnect your Gmail account at any time from Settings, which stops any further access immediately.

Your connected Google Drive (optional). If you choose to import your unit inventory from a Google Sheet, AgentEasy requests read-only access to Google Drive and Sheets to read that spreadsheet's contents. We don't access any other files in your Drive.

Device permissions (optional). With your permission, AgentEasy can access:

  • Contacts — to save a lead or client as a contact on your device.
  • Location — to center the Units map search on where you are.
  • Calendar — to add your scheduled showings as calendar events with reminders.

Each of these is requested only when you use the matching feature, and you can revoke any of them at any time from your device's Settings app.

Push notifications. We store a device push token so we can notify you about new leads, client replies, and showing reminders. You can turn notification types on or off from within AgentEasy's Settings.

Lead and client data. As part of using AgentEasy, you (or messages that arrive through your connected inbox or imported spreadsheets) will add information about your own prospective tenants and clients — names, emails, phone numbers, and details they've shared about their inquiry. You control this data and are responsible for handling it in accordance with applicable law; AgentEasy stores it on your behalf so you can manage your pipeline.

Diagnostics. If the app crashes or you submit feedback from within the app, we collect a basic error report or your message so we can fix the underlying issue.

How we use this information

We use the information above to operate AgentEasy's core features: importing and organizing leads, sending and tracking email replies you've authorized, syncing your unit inventory, scheduling showings, sending push notifications, and diagnosing bugs. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it.

Sharing

We share information only with the services that power AgentEasy, and only as needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication.
  • Google — for the Gmail and Drive features you explicitly connect.
  • Apple and Google's push notification services — to deliver notifications to your device.

We may also disclose information if required to by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of AgentEasy, our users, or others.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and its associated data at any time from Account → Delete Account within the app.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and database-level access controls that restrict data to its owner.

Children's privacy

AgentEasy is intended for licensed and aspiring real estate professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@hazbot.app.
